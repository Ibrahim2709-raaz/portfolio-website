What I carry with me

Reflection gives technology direction.

Growing up in Pakistan gave me a relationship with poetry, music, and thoughtful conversation. Studying computer science in Canada gave those instincts a new medium: systems, interfaces, research, and code.

I do not see those parts of myself as separate. Poetry teaches me that every word has weight. Piano teaches patience, rhythm, and how individual notes become a whole. Critical reflection helps me slow down long enough to find the real problem before I build the solution.
