About Me

Get to know my journey, passions, and work beyond the screen.

Background

I’m Ibrahim, a Computer Science student driven by curiosity and creativity. My journey has always been about blending logic with imagination—whether it’s building software, solving problems, or expressing through writing. Each step forward is a way to explore both the digital and human sides of life.

Education

Currently pursuing my studies in Computer Science, I’ve explored areas such as data structures, optimization techniques, algorithms, and AI. My academic journey reflects not only technical skills but also critical thinking, teamwork, and a strong commitment to lifelong learning.

Nonprofit & Community Work

Outside academics, I founded and contribute to SERPA, a nonprofit initiative that raises awareness in children’s libraries and youth centers. Over the past six months, SERPA has reached 1,000+ people through informational campaigns, fundraising efforts, and youth-centered projects. For me, tech and compassion go hand in hand.

Beyond Work

Beyond school and projects, I find meaning in poetry, storytelling, and exploring cultural connections. My hobbies allow me to balance the analytical with the creative, keeping me grounded and inspired. Whether it’s writing verses or reflecting on philosophy, I aim to connect deeply with ideas and people alike.
